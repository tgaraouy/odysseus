Odysseus

A sovereign agent platform on a sealed, hash-chained record. The mark is the flagship sibling of aep/v1.1 — same paper, same ink, the one seal red.

01

The seal

One glyph, three meanings: a compass / north star (the journey, the navigating agent), a dashed ring (the chain of blocks — tamper-evident), and a seal-red core (the genesis block — the sovereign, sealed origin).
