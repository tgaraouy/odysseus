# Gout Intervention Diet — low-carb / high-fat

A **medium-term intervention** to kick-start recovery — followed for as long as necessary, then
relaxed on your own terms once healing progresses. It is deliberately **not flexible** the way a
long-term lifestyle diet is. Educational/organizational, not medical advice. **Allopurinol 100mg
stays as your clinician directs.**

## Required alongside the diet (the source is explicit: diet alone is unlikely to work)
- **Stress management** — to lower cortisol (cortisol is inflammatory).
- **Better sleep** — earlier dim lights, no screens before bed, a set bedtime.
- **Therapeutic exercise** — walking, gardening, gentle movement; doesn't need to be hard.
- **No alcohol, no tobacco.** *(You already don't drink — that lever is done.)*

## The 4 aims
Bring down **INFLAMMATION** · lose **WEIGHT** · lower **URIC-ACID PRODUCTION** · improve **KIDNEY FUNCTION**.

## Eat
- **Fatty proteins** — red meat (mince/ground beef or patties are higher-fat, preferred), chicken,
  turkey; avoid highly processed products. **Oily fish** (salmon, sardines, cold-water). **Eggs.**
  The best protein foods carry their own fat/oil — you need it for energy without sugar, and to
  stop protein being burned for energy. **Avoid lean meats; no protein powders/supplements.**
- **Full-fat dairy** — cheese, cream, butter, raw yogurt. **No milk** (sugar). **Never fat-free.**
- **Nuts** — walnuts, pecans, almonds. **Not peanuts** (legume, inflammatory).
- **Vegetables** — mostly leafy greens; cruciferous best (cabbage, broccoli, brussels sprouts,
  cauliflower, artichoke), plus asparagus and leeks. **Green salads**, cucumber, lettuce.
- **Avocados & olives** (low sugar, high fat) — the only "fruit" to eat.
- **Seeds** — pumpkin, chia, flax.
- **Aromatics** — turmeric (curcumin), ginger, garlic (anti-inflammatory) in cooking.
- **Fats for cooking** — butter or lard; **add olive oil to every meal before eating**.
- **Omega-3 oil** supplement (anti-inflammatory).
- **Fluids + electrolytes** — drink adequately for kidney function; electrolyte with **potassium +
  magnesium**; **salt food to taste** (your body knows).

## Avoid
- **Sugars / carbohydrates / starches** — no bread, pasta, cereal, potatoes, rice, corn, oats,
  wheat; no desserts, cakes, cookies, candies, sodas, ice-cream, sweetened dairy. (Sugar → insulin
  → inflammation, and blocks weight loss.)
- **Processed foods** (often injected with fructose — likely the single biggest driver of uric acid).
- **Fruit** (high fructose) — except avocado/olives.
- **Legumes** (beans, lentils, peas, peanuts) and **nightshades** (peppers, tomatoes, eggplant) —
  can be inflammatory.

## Carnivore variant (optional)
You may skip the vegetables — but then it's **critical to get enough Vitamin C**, so make sure
**seafood and organ meats** feature regularly.

## Research notes (additive, not changes to the rules above)
- **First 2–3 weeks: urate may transiently rise** — ketones compete with uric acid for kidney
  excretion. Ramp carbs down gradually, hydrate hard, keep electrolytes up; an early flare in this
  window is a known transition effect, not failure. ([RCT meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10307853/))
- **Why this may help inflammation regardless of urate:** the ketone **BHB blocks the NLRP3
  inflammasome**, the pathway that drives crystal flares — so the anti-inflammatory effect doesn't
  require high urate (relevant to your normouricemic pattern). ([Cell Reports 2017](https://www.cell.com/cell-reports/fulltext/S2211-1247(17)30169-9))

## Make it *your* data (n-of-1)
- **Baseline (logged 2026-06-12):** weight 194 lb, no flare (right-Achilles soreness), home urate
  5.2 avg; WHOOP recovery 43%, HRV 20.9, RHR 61, sleep 9.76h.
- **During:** journal daily — weight, flare y/n + which joint + severity, home urate when tested.
- **Read at ~3–4 weeks and ~8 weeks:** did flare frequency / severity / duration move? weight,
  urate, HRV, sleep? That's your signal — mechanism-agnostic.
