# Low-Carb / High-Fat Intervention — gout/flare protocol

Built from the two SHAPEFIXER transcripts you pasted + a quick research pass. This is a
medium-term **intervention** (not a forever lifestyle), to be relaxed once flares settle.
Educational/organizational — not medical advice. **Allopurinol 100mg stays as your clinician
directs; nothing here changes it.** You already don't drink alcohol, so that lever is done.

## The 4 aims (from the transcripts)
Lower **inflammation** · lower **weight** · lower **uric-acid production** · support **kidney function**.

## The single most important rule
**High FAT, *adequate* protein — not high lean protein.** Fat is the energy source here; if you
go low-carb but also low-fat, the body has to make glucose from protein, which is the failure
mode the videos warn about. Eat fat *with* every protein. Skip protein powders and very lean cuts.

## Eat
- **Fatty proteins:** ground beef / patties, fattier cuts of beef/pork/lamb, chicken *with skin*,
  **oily fish** (salmon, sardines, mackerel), **eggs**.
- **Fats:** butter, lard/tallow, **olive oil added to meals**, avocado oil, coconut/MCT oil,
  cheese, cream, full-fat or raw yogurt, **avocado, olives**.
- **Low-sugar veg:** leafy greens + cruciferous (broccoli, **cauliflower**, cabbage, brussels
  sprouts), asparagus, leeks, artichoke, cucumber, green salads.
- **Nuts/seeds:** walnuts, pecans, almonds; pumpkin, chia, flax.
- **Anti-inflammatory aromatics:** turmeric, ginger, garlic.
- **Omega-3:** oily fish and/or a supplement (anti-inflammatory).
- **Fluids + electrolytes:** drink well; add potassium + magnesium; salt to taste.

## Avoid (medium-term)
- **Sugar & fructose:** sodas, sweets, desserts, anything processed/sweetened.
- **Grains/starch:** bread, pasta, rice, oats, corn, wheat, potatoes.
- **Fruit** (fructose) — except avocado/olives. *(Evidence-based exception: tart cherries are
  associated with* fewer *flares — optional add if you want one.)*
- **Protein powders / very lean meat without fat.**

## Cut-but-your-call (the videos say avoid; evidence is weaker — I'm not adjudicating)
- **Seed/vegetable oils** — the videos call them toxic; high-quality evidence is mixed. Easy to
  cut anyway since you're cooking in butter/olive oil.
- **Legumes & nightshades** — claimed inflammatory, but large gout data found *plant* purines and
  vegetables **not** linked to flares. Cut if you personally react; otherwise optional.

## Cautions that actually matter (research-grounded)
- **First 2–3 weeks: urate may transiently rise** as ketones compete with uric acid for kidney
  excretion → ramp carbs down gradually, hydrate hard, keep electrolytes up. A flare in this
  window is a known transition effect, not a sign it's failing. (Allopurinol on board may blunt it.)
- **Why it may help your inflammation regardless of urate:** the ketone **BHB blocks the NLRP3
  inflammasome**, the pathway that drives crystal flares — so the anti-inflammatory effect doesn't
  depend on your urate being high. This is the part most relevant to your normouricemic pattern.

## Make it *your* data (the point of your ledger)
You said past protocols helped you — this run should be measured, not remembered:
- **Before starting:** log a baseline — home urate, weight, current flare state, recent WHOOP
  (recovery/HRV/sleep).
- **During:** journal daily (the existing fields: weight, flare y/n, home urate when you test,
  mood/energy); note any flare's day/severity/duration.
- **Read it at 3–4 weeks and 8 weeks:** did flare *frequency/severity/duration* move? did weight,
  HRV, sleep move? That's your n-of-1 signal — mechanism-agnostic.

## A simple day (template, adjust freely)
- **Meal 1:** eggs cooked in butter + avocado; coffee with butter/cream if you want.
- **Meal 2:** big green salad with olive oil + a fatty protein (salmon / ground beef / chicken thigh).
- **Meal 3:** fatty meat or oily fish + cruciferous veg roasted in tallow/olive oil.
- **Throughout:** water + electrolytes; omega-3; salt to taste.
