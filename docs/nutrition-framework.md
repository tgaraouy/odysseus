# Nutrition — holistic framework (flexible, not a fixed plan)

One way of eating that serves **gout, inflammation, heart, cholesterol, allergies/MCAS, and
aging** together — fit to how you actually eat: **cook at home, creative, eating window after
~12pm, no fixed routine.** Educational/organizational, not medical advice. Allopurinol stays
as your clinician directs.

This replaces the rigid weekly meal plan. The method: maximize what's robustly good across
*every* axis, and surface the two real trade-offs as **dials you set from your own data** —
not a one-size diet.

## Why "holistic" ≠ "the gout plan, flexibly"
The earlier gout protocol (high saturated fat, butter/tallow/ribeye, lots of red meat, aged
cheese, cured/canned fish) optimizes for **urate + insulin** but actively conflicts with two of
your other axes:
- **Heart / cholesterol (ApoB):** heavy saturated fat + red meat pushes the wrong way.
- **Allergies / MCAS:** aged cheese, cured/smoked meat, canned fish, fermented, leftovers,
  spinach, tomato, eggplant, avocado, olives are **high-histamine**.

So the framework keeps the gout/insulin wins (low sugar, high-quality fat) but shifts the fat
emphasis and freshness to also serve heart and histamine.

## The core — good across every axis
- **Minimal sugar / refined carbs / starch.** The single rule that helps *all* of it: urate
  (fructose), insulin & inflammation, heart, glycation/aging, metabolic/glycemic. Non-negotiable.
- **Primary fats: olive oil + omega-3 from FRESH oily fish.** Heart-friendly, anti-inflammatory,
  low-histamine. (Monounsaturated + omega-3 over heavy saturated.)
- **Eat fresh — cooked and eaten the same day.** Minimizes histamine: skip leftovers, aged,
  cured, canned, fermented.
- **Plants: leafy greens + cruciferous** (broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage, brussels) — fiber,
  low sugar, anti-inflammatory.
- **Aromatics:** turmeric, ginger, garlic. **Hydration** + electrolytes (K/Mg) + salt to taste.

## The two dials (you set them from your data)
**① Saturated fat & red meat ↔ heart / ApoB.**
Default low: lean on olive oil, avocado, fish, poultry; red meat occasional. Turn it up only if
your lipids are clean and gout/insulin is the binding constraint. *Rule: your ApoB/LDL sets this,
not a guru.* If ApoB is elevated → keep it down.

**② Histamine (MCAS) ↔ allergies / inflammation.**
When symptoms are up → fresh meat/fish cooked & eaten same day; cut aged cheese, cured/smoked
meat, canned fish, fermented, leftovers, and the high-histamine plants (spinach, tomato,
eggplant, and for some, avocado/olives). Relax when calm. *Rule: flare → tighten; calm → relax.*

## Build-a-plate (after 12pm — pick and improvise)
| layer | pick from (fresh, home) |
|---|---|
| protein | fresh fish (salmon, white fish), poultry, eggs · occasional fresh red meat |
| fat | olive oil (default) · avocado* · some butter/ghee  (*histamine-dependent) |
| vegetables (2+) | leafy greens, cruciferous, asparagus, leeks, cucumber, zucchini |
| aromatics | garlic, ginger, turmeric, fresh herbs, lemon |
| assemble | sheet-pan · stir-fry · big salad-bowl · soup — same parts, endless variations |

Your window is ~12pm onward, so ~2 meals (± a small third). No breakfast needed.

## Late-day sugar craving
- Make each meal **fat + protein dense** so satiety carries — the craving is usually a blood-sugar
  dip or too little protein/fat earlier in the window.
- Keep a non-sugar off-ramp: a few berries (if histamine ok) · a square of 85%+ dark chocolate ·
  cinnamon / herbal tea.
- **Close the eating window before the craving's usual peak.** If no eating window is open when
  it hits, the loop has nowhere to go.
- Log when it hits + what preceded it (the journal on `/health`) → find the trigger, then design
  it out.

## Make it your data (n-of-1)
Track and let the dials follow the evidence:
- **Symptoms:** gout flares, histamine/MCAS symptoms, energy.
- **Labs:** urate, ApoB/lipid panel, inflammation markers (ESR/CRP), glucose/metabolic.
- **The sugar-craving pattern** (timing + trigger), weight.

Adjust dial ① by your lipids, dial ② by your histamine symptoms. The framework is fixed; the
two dials move with what your body and labs show.

## To personalize further
Give the agent your **specific allergens** and **latest ApoB/lipid numbers** and it will set the
dials and tailor the build-a-plate to you. The gout-specific lens lives in
